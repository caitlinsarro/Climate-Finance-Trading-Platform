# CFP-2026-001 — Decision

## Engineering decision: Accepted

The governance change is **Accepted** because the constitution now requires
mathematical definitions, units, sign conventions, timing, aggregation,
calibration evidence, uncertainty, reconciliation, and honest output semantics
for physical and transition risk.

The feature does not change model calculations. Its acceptance confirms that
the governance requirement was implemented and validated; it does not certify
the current model's empirical validity.

## Model-promotion decision: Not promoted

The current physical- and transition-risk models remain **research-only**.
Production promotion is prohibited because:

- the official climate cache contains a material fallback-labeling defect;
- scenario and issuer coefficients are predominantly project calibrations;
- transition channels do not yet reconcile from issuer physical units into
  financial units;
- tactical physical risk is not geospatially or empirically calibrated to
  issuer financial loss;
- independent external validation has not been completed.

## Required follow-up

Create and complete CFP-2026-002 to remediate provenance labeling, eliminate
future or fabricated fallback observations, introduce field-level source
metadata, add dimensional and external-calibration tests, and restrict output
labels to the units supported by evidence.
